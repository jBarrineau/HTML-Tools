    Connect & verify not connected

    One click calls WhoAmI against the endpoint above. That both proves the tool is authenticated and captures your owner GUID — no separate sign-in-and-paste step.

    Auth: in its SPFx host the tool uses, in order — (1) a token from window.getDataverseToken(resourceOrigin) if the host page defines it (wire this to the SPFx AadHttpClient/token provider), (2) an access token pasted in Advanced below, or (3) the ambient session cookies (credentials:"include"). Every stage also keeps a manual-paste fallback, so a blocked call never dead-ends the wizard.
